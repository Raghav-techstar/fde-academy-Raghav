# Day 18 - Exercise 3
# Stakeholder Update Email for a Delayed Integration

################################
## Task 1 - Executive Version
################################

**Subject: Integration Completion Moved to Tuesday - No Action Needed**

Hi [Steering Committee],

The system integration will now be completed Tuesday instead of Friday due to an unexpected authentication complexity discovered during implementation.

- The issue is being addressed and does not affect current operations.
- The team is working through the required fix and validation.
- Tuesday is the current expected completion date.

No action is needed from your team at this time. We will provide another update once validation is complete.

Rajesh


######################################
## Task 2 - Technical Lead Version
######################################

**Subject: Integration Completion Delayed to Tuesday - Authentication Issue**

Hi [Technical Lead Name],

The system integration is now expected to complete Tuesday instead of Friday due to an authentication complexity discovered during implementation.

The issue was identified during the authentication portion of the integration. We are addressing the issue and will complete the required testing and validation before final integration.

The revised plan is:

- Complete the authentication fix.
- Run the updated integration tests.
- Complete final validation by Tuesday.

No action is required from your team at this time. We will share the updated test results once validation is complete.

Rajesh

#################################
## Task 3 - Channel and RACI
#################################

### Channel Choice

**Steering Committee Version:** Email

**Why:**
The schedule change is a formal project update and needs a clear written record for the stakeholders.

**Technical Lead Version:** Email

**Why:**
The technical lead needs the revised timeline and technical details in a written format that can be referred to during implementation.


## RACI - Accept the New Tuesday Date

**R - Responsible:** FDE Technical Lead

**A - Accountable:** Client IT Director

**C - Consulted:** Client Technical Lead

**I - Informed:** Steering Committee


#########################
# Reflection Questions
#########################

## A1.

Yes. The executive version focuses on the new date, business impact, and whether any action is needed. The technical version contains more detail about authentication, testing, and validation.

## A2.

It could create confusion about who actually owns the decision. Both people might assume that the other person is responsible for accepting the new date.

## A3.

I would still use email because another delay changes the project commitment and needs a clear record. I would also consider a meeting if the repeated delay required discussion or a decision from the stakeholders.
